# One Star, Five Stars for the Food

One sentence, in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review — while the street food quietly wins the argument.

> One star: Earth charges for water, hands out opinions free, and its customer service never answers — yet the street tacos were so good I filed my complaint in salsa instead of ink.
